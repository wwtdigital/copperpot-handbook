# Copperpot Bowls: copperpot-handbook

> **Fictional company.** Copperpot Bowls is a fictional company. This repository, its history and its data were generated by WWT Digital to demonstrate the Agentic Readiness Scorecard. No real company, person or system is behind it.

Copperpot Bowls engineering handbook: standards, onboarding, architecture decision records, retro pages and incident timelines as Markdown, kept beside the code. The wiki is the system of record. Fictional demonstration estate.

---

## What this is

The engineering handbook: the standards, onboarding notes, architecture decision records, retro pages and incident timelines the digital platform teams keep beside the code. The commerce team owns the repository; every team writes in it.

**The wiki is the system of record.** The Engineering Standards space holds the current text of every standard and the Commerce Team Home space holds onboarding. This repository is the copy engineers read in their editor and link from pull requests. When the two disagree, the wiki wins, and the page here gets a change.

## Layout

| Folder | Holds |
|---|---|
| `standards/` | one page per standard, with the date it took effect in the front matter |
| `onboarding/` | the first-week page and where it points |
| `adr/` | architecture decision records, MADR format, numbered |
| `retros/` | monthly retro pages with their actions |
| `incidents/` | one timeline page per incident |

## Writing in it

- One change per pull request, reviewed by someone on the owning team of the page.
- Dates are written `YYYY-MM-DD`. Times carry the store's offset.
- Name roles, not people: "the payments lead", not a name.
- A standard changes by a new `effective` date and a line in the page's history table, never by a silent edit.
